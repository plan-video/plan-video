Post videos and photo posts to TikTok.

## What you can post

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Caption | 4,000 characters |
| Video | Up to 200 MB, and as long as your TikTok account allows (up to 5 minutes in plan.video) |
| Photo post | Up to 35 pictures |

## Options

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Send it">
    **To the profile** posts the video. **To the inbox** sends it to your TikTok inbox instead: open the notification in TikTok to edit it and post it yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can watch">
    **Everyone**, **Friends**, **Followers** or **Only me**. TikTok asks you to choose each time, so there's no default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Allow users to">
    **Comment**, **Duet** and **Stitch** are off unless you turn them on. Duet and Stitch are for videos only. If one is turned off in your TikTok privacy settings, you can't turn it on here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Disclose commercial content">
    Turn this on if the post promotes you, a brand, a product or a service, then pick **Your brand** or **Branded content**. Branded content can't be set to **Only me**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
TikTok adds a trending sound to photo posts. Change it in the TikTok app once the post is up.
</Note>
