The **Assistant** is the chat built into the dashboard. Tell it what you want to post, and it writes, plans and schedules it for you.

## Open it

Click the round button at the bottom right of any dashboard page, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> on Windows). **Assistant** in the side rail opens it as a full page.

Start from a suggestion like **Plan a week of posts** or **Find my best times**, or just ask in your own words. Then:

- **Channels** picks the accounts it should post to.
- **Insert media** attaches up to 4 files from your media library.
- **Chats** brings back an earlier chat, and **New chat** starts over.

## What it can do

- See your connected accounts and each network's rules
- Read your [Analytics](/docs/analytics) and pick good times
- Write posts, and save them as drafts or schedule them
- Copy, change and delete posts
- Publish a post when you ask

## What it can't do

- Make pictures or videos, or edit them. Attach the files you want to use.
- Find files by itself. It only sees what you attach.
- Change a post that has gone out. The network already has it.

## When it asks first

It always asks in the chat before it publishes or deletes. It also asks before it saves a post that's due within 10 minutes, or one going to a TikTok profile. A post scheduled further ahead is saved without asking, and you'll see it on your calendar.

## Limits

How much you can use it each day depends on your plan: least on Free, and more on each plan above. When you reach the limit, it says so and you can carry on the next day.

Your chats are your own, kept per workspace. What you ask goes to Anthropic to answer. A chat with YouTube numbers in it is deleted 30 days after its last message.
