Every post starts in **New post** in the dashboard.

## Write the post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick accounts">
    Choose the accounts to post to, or an account group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Add a video or up to 35 pictures from your computer or your [media library](/docs/media-library). You can also attach a project from the editor; its export is added for you. Click a picture to crop, rotate or flip it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the caption">
    One caption goes to every network. Open a network's tab to give it its own caption, media or options instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the previews">
    Each network shows a live preview, and warns you when something won't fit, like a caption that's too long.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Captions for each network

Each network has its own caption limit. plan.video counts an emoji as one character, as the networks do.

| Network | Caption limit |
| --- | --- |
| TikTok | 4,000 |
| YouTube | 5,000, plus a title of up to 100 |
| X | 280 |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 |
| Bluesky | 300 |
| Instagram | 2,200 |
| Facebook | 63,206 |
| Threads | 500 |

See [Networks](/docs/networks) for every limit.

## Network options

Some networks have settings of their own, like TikTok's **Who can watch** or YouTube's title and privacy. They show under **Platform settings**, below the caption, with a tab for each network. See each network's page for what they do.

## Threads

Under the caption, click **Add a follow-up** to post more under the post once it's out, like a link kept out of the caption or the next part of a story. A post can have up to 10. Each goes out a set time after the one before it, from right away up to a day later. On X and Bluesky a follow-up can also carry up to 4 pictures or a video: click the media button under it.

| Network | Follow-ups go out as | Limit | Media |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | Replies, each under the one before | 280 | 4 pictures or a video |
| Bluesky | Replies, each under the one before | 300 | 4 pictures or a video up to 100 MB |
| LinkedIn | Comments on the post | 1,250 | Words only |

Other networks get the post alone. If a follow-up fails, the ones after it don't go out, and you get an email saying why.

## Saved text in the Vault

On paid plans, save captions, hashtags and snippets you use often in the **Vault**, then drop them into any caption from the **Vault** button. See [Vault and templates](/docs/vault-and-templates), which also covers starting a post from a template.

## Stories and covers

- **Stories** go to Instagram and Facebook, with one picture or one video of up to 60 seconds and no caption.
- **Covers:** pick the frame to use as a video's cover. A single Instagram Reel uses it. TikTok may show the video's first frame whatever you pick.

## Save, schedule or post

When the post is ready, choose when it goes out. See [Schedule and repeat](/docs/scheduling). To come back to it later, click **Save draft**.
