Every post starts in **New post** in the dashboard.

## Write the post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick accounts">
    Choose the accounts to post to, or an account group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Add a video or up to 35 pictures from your computer or your [media library](/docs/media-library). You can also attach a project from the editor; its export is added for you. Click a picture to crop, rotate or flip it. Click **Quick edit** on a video to trim, crop or mute it without opening the editor. The file's name fills in an empty title and caption, so `My trip to Lisbon.mp4` starts as "My trip to Lisbon". Names a phone or app made up, like `IMG_4821`, are left out. Turn this off under [Preferences](/docs/preferences#new-post).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the caption">
    One caption goes to every network. On YouTube it's the video's description, and a **Title** field shows above it. Open a network's tab to give it its own caption, media or options instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the previews">
    Each network shows a live preview, and warns you when something won't fit, like a caption that's too long.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Clips from a stream

Open **Projects → Stream Clipper** and paste the link to a past stream on Twitch, Kick or YouTube, or a channel's link for its latest one. plan.video reads the stream's chat, how loud it gets, and what viewers clipped or replayed most, writes down what was said in the parts that stand out, and picks up to 30 moments that work on their own. A 2-hour stream takes about a minute; keep the page open while it works.

Each clip opens in its own editor, with a 9:16 preview that plays as the post will look:

- **Trim:** drag the ends of the bar under the preview, or press `[` and `]` to start or end at the playhead. A clip can grow past the moment that was picked.
- **Layout:** pick a layout, then drag the boxes over the stream to choose what shows where. plan.video finds the facecam for you.
- **Captions:** choose a style, size, colour, position and how many words show at once, and fix any word that was heard wrong.
- **Hook:** the big line over the first seconds: its words, size, position and how long it stays.
- **Post:** the title and caption the post starts with, and exact start and end times.

When a clip is ready, **Schedule** opens it in New post, **Download** saves the video, and **Open in editor** makes it a project you can change in every way the editor allows. **Add a clip** picks any other stretch of the stream by hand. Your clips and changes are kept on this device.

- The stream has to be public. A Twitch broadcast for subscribers only, a YouTube video for members only, and an age-restricted YouTube video can't be read.
- A YouTube stream that's still live can be clipped once it has ended.
- Finding the moments uses about 35 minutes of your monthly [captions](/docs/captions), however long the stream. When fewer are left, plan.video checks the best parts it has room for.
- Parts Twitch muted for music are skipped.

## Captions for each network

Each network has its own caption limit. plan.video counts characters the way each network does: an emoji is one character, except on X, which counts an emoji, and a Chinese, Japanese or Korean character, as two, and any link as 23.

| Network | Caption limit |
| --- | --- |
| TikTok | 4,000 |
| YouTube | 5,000, plus a title of up to 100 |
| X | 280 |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 |
| Bluesky | 300 |
| Instagram | 2,200 |
| Facebook | 63,206 |
| Threads | 500 |

See [Networks](/docs/networks) for every limit.

## Network options

Some networks have settings of their own, like TikTok's **Who can watch** or YouTube's privacy. They show under **Platform settings**, below the caption, with a tab for each network. See each network's page for what they do.

## Threads

Under the caption, click **Add a follow-up** to post more under the post once it's out, like a link kept out of the caption or the next part of a story. A post can have up to 10. Each goes out a set time after the one before it, from right away up to a day later. On X and Bluesky a follow-up can also carry up to 4 pictures or a video: click the media button under it.

| Network | Follow-ups go out as | Limit | Media |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | Replies, each under the one before | 280 | 4 pictures or a video |
| Bluesky | Replies, each under the one before | 300 | 4 pictures or a video up to 100 MB |
| LinkedIn | Comments on the post | 1,250 | Words only |

Other networks get the post alone. If a follow-up fails, the ones after it don't go out, and you get an email saying why.

## Saved text in the Vault

On paid plans, save captions, hashtags and snippets you use often in the **Vault**, then drop them into any caption from the **Vault** button. See [Vault and templates](/docs/vault-and-templates), which also covers starting a post from a template.

## Stories and covers

- **Stories** go to Instagram and Facebook, with one picture or one video of up to 60 seconds and no caption.
- **Covers:** pick the frame to use as a video's cover. A single Instagram Reel uses it. TikTok may show the video's first frame whatever you pick.

## Save, schedule or post

When the post is ready, choose when it goes out. See [Schedule and repeat](/docs/scheduling). To come back to it later, click **Save draft**.
