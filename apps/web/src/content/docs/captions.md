Add captions to your video in the editor, style them, and make them your own.

## Auto-captions

Select a video clip or the scene, then click **Auto-Captions** on the toolbar above the canvas. plan.video writes captions from the speech, and only the audio is sent to be transcribed.

| Plan | Auto-captions a month |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 15 minutes |
| Creator | 10 hours |
| Pro | 20 hours |
| Agency | 40 hours |

Your time resets on the 1st. A clip longer than what you have left is turned down.

## Styles

Select the captions, pick a look under **Caption** in the inspector (**All looks** shows every one), then change the font, colours and position to fit your video.

### Brand style

Click **Save** beside **Brand** to keep a caption look as your workspace's brand style. New captions start in it, and **Apply** brings it back to captions you've already made.

## Caption tools

Find these under **Caption tools** in the inspector when captions are selected. Emoji, keywords and translation send the captions' words to Anthropic, and have a daily limit that depends on your plan.

## Fix a word

Select the captions and open **Words** in the inspector. You can change a word, make it **Shorter** or **Longer**, or **Remove word**. That only changes the captions: to take a word out of the video itself, see [Cut the transcript](/docs/cut-the-transcript).
