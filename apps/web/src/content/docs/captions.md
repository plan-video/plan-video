Add captions to your video in the editor, style them, and make them your own.

## Auto-captions

plan.video writes captions from your video's speech. Only the audio is sent to be transcribed. Your plan includes a number of caption minutes each month, which reset on the 1st.

## Styles

Pick one of the caption styles, then change the font, colours and position to fit your video.

### Brand style

Save a caption look as your workspace's brand style, and new captions start in it.

## Caption tools

Find these in the inspector when captions are selected:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Add emoji" icon="message-square">
    Adds fitting emoji to your captions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Highlight keywords" icon="hash">
    Picks out the key words and colours them in the colour you choose.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Translate" icon="languages">
    Translates your captions into another language.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Download .srt" icon="captions">
    Saves your captions as a subtitle file for other apps.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Fix a word

Click a caption to change its words or timing. To take a word out of the video itself, see [Cut the transcript](/docs/cut-the-transcript).
