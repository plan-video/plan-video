Post to your Threads profile with text, pictures or a video.

## What you can post

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | 500 characters |
| Pictures | Up to 20 in a carousel |
| Video | One per post, up to 5 minutes and 500 MB |

## Good to know

- A post's link shows as a preview when the post is text only.
- Threads takes up to **250 posts a day** from one profile.
- Your Threads login lasts **60 days** and renews by itself while plan.video posts or reads your numbers. After two months without either, connect the account again in **Settings** → **Connections**.
