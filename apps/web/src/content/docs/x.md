Post to X with text, pictures or a video.

## What you can post

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | 280 characters |
| Pictures | Up to 4, 5 MB each |
| Video | Up to 500 MB and 5 minutes in plan.video |

## Good to know

- **Paid plans only.** X charges for posting through its API, so X is available from the Creator plan.
- **Posts a month.** X charges plan.video for every post, and far more for a post with a link, so each plan posts to X up to a set number of times in any 30 days, and fewer of those can have a link:

  | | Posts to X | With a link |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Creator | 150 | 10 |
  | Pro | 400 | 25 |
  | Agency | 800 | 40 |

  These count every X account you've connected together, and follow-ups and plugs count too. A post past either limit isn't sent, and its error says when there's room again. To send one past the link limit, take the link out and post it again.
- **Links include bare addresses.** A post counts as having a link when it has a web address in it, with or without `https://`, such as `plan.video`.
- **50 posts a day.** You can post to X up to 50 times in any 24 hours. A post past that waits, and goes out on its own once there's room. Rarely, a video that finds the day used up only after its upload fails after 2 hours; click **Retry**.
- **Who can reply.** Choose **Everyone**, **Following** or **Mentioned** in the post's X settings. It applies to the post, not its follow-ups.
- **Communities.** Paste a community's link to post there instead of your timeline. You have to be a member, and its members can reply.

## Plugs

Plugs act on your X posts once they take off. When a post reaches the number of likes you set, plan.video can **repost** it or **reply** to it, for example with a link. Posts are checked 6, 12 and 18 hours after they go out, and a reply can be 3 to 280 characters. Only plugs turned on before a post went out apply to it, and a post that reaches the number after the last check gets nothing.

Each repost and reply counts as one of your plan's posts to X, and a reply with a link counts toward its links too. A plug past either limit isn't done.

Set them up in **Plugs** in the dashboard.
