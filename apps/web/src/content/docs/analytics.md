**Analytics**, in the dashboard's side rail, shows how your posts do. It's in beta.

## Which networks

Analytics has numbers for **TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Pages and Threads**. X, LinkedIn and Bluesky don't show numbers here yet.

## What's in it

| Page | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Your views, likes, comments and engagement over time |
| Posts | Each post and how it did |
| Platforms | Each network side by side |
| Best times | The days and hours your posts do best |

Pick a range, from today to all time or dates of your own, and it compares them with the period before. You can also look at one account from the list of accounts.

## Post at your best times

**Best times** uses your posts from the last six months, in your time zone. Click **Add to Auto-schedule** on a time to add it to [Auto-schedule](/docs/scheduling#auto-schedule).

New post offers **Best for your audience** when you pick a time, and [Bulk tools](/docs/bulk-tools) can spread a batch over your best times.
