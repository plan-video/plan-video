Post videos and Shorts to YouTube.

## What you can post

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Required, up to 100 characters. Titles over 60 may be cut off in search. |
| Description | 5,000 characters |
| Video | Up to 500 MB and 5 minutes in plan.video |
| Tags | 500 characters in all, 30 characters each |

## Shorts

A video shaped **9:16** and **3 minutes or shorter** is posted as a Short.

## Uploads a day

Each plan uploads a set number of videos a day: 3 on Free, 50 on Creator, 100 on Pro and 200 on Agency. A video past that waits, and goes out on its own once there's room.

## Options

- **Privacy status:** public, unlisted or private.
- **Made for kids:** turn on if the video is made for children.
- **Tags:** words that help people find the video.
