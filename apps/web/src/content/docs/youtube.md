Post videos and Shorts to YouTube.

## What you can post

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Required, up to 100 characters. Titles over 60 may be cut off in search. |
| Description | 5,000 characters |
| Video | Up to 2 GB, and up to 5 minutes in plan.video |
| Tags | 500 characters in all |

## Shorts

A video shaped **9:16** and **3 minutes or shorter** is posted as a Short.

## Options

- **Visibility:** public, unlisted or private.
- **Made for kids:** turn on if the video is made for children.
- **Tags:** words that help people find the video.

<Note>
Until Google finishes reviewing plan.video, YouTube keeps uploads private. Change the visibility in YouTube Studio once the video is up.
</Note>
