Upload videos and pictures to **Media** once, then use them in any post.

## What you can upload

| Type | Formats | Largest file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video | MP4, MOV | 500 MB |
| Picture | JPG, PNG | 8 MB |

## Video rules

So a video can go to every network, plan.video checks that it is:

- between **3 seconds and 5 minutes** long
- **9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:3**

A post carries one video or up to 35 pictures, 500 MB in all. Each network can have tighter limits; see [Networks](/docs/networks).

## Edit a picture

Click a picture in a post, or **Edit** on a picture in Media, to crop, rotate or flip it. Pick a shape like **1:1**, **4:5** or **9:16** to keep the crop in that shape, then drag it to choose what stays.

- In a post, the edit only changes that post. While the post is open, click the picture again to change the crop, or **Reset** to go back to the picture as it was.
- In Media, **Save copy** keeps the original and adds the edited picture next to it.

## Storage

Your files count toward your plan's storage. You can see how much you use under **Settings** → **Storage**.
