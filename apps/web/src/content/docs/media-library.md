Upload videos and pictures to **Media**, under **Library** in the dashboard, once. Then use them in any post.

## What you can upload

| Type | Formats | Largest file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video | MP4, MOV | 500 MB |
| Picture | JPG, PNG | 8 MB |

## From Google Drive

Click **Google Drive** next to **Upload**, sign in to Google, and pick your files. plan.video copies them into Media then, so moving or deleting them in Drive later changes nothing here. plan.video only sees the files you pick. The same button is in **New post** and in the editor's Assets panel.

## Video rules

plan.video checks that a video is:

- between **3 seconds and 5 minutes** long
- **9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:3**

A post carries one video or up to 35 pictures, 500 MB in all. Each network can have tighter limits; see [Networks](/docs/networks).

## Edit a picture

Click a picture in a post, or **Edit** on a picture in Media, to crop, rotate or flip it. Pick a shape like **1:1**, **4:5** or **9:16** to keep the crop in that shape, then drag it to choose what stays.

**Remove background** takes the background out and leaves your subject on a see-through PNG. It runs on your device, so nothing is uploaded, and the first picture downloads the model once. Click it again to bring the background back. Facebook, Instagram, Threads and TikTok don't take see-through pictures, so there it goes out on white.

- In a post, the edit only changes that post. While the post is open, click the picture again to change the crop, or **Reset** to go back to the picture as it was.
- In Media, **Save copy** keeps the original and adds the edited picture next to it.

## Edit a video

In a post, click **Quick edit** on a video to make it fit without opening the editor:

- **Trim:** drag the handles at each end of the strip to keep only the part you want. It stays at least 3 seconds long.
- **Crop:** pick **9:16**, **16:9**, **1:1** or **4:3**, then drag the frame to choose what stays. A video in a shape networks don't take opens already cropped to the closest one.
- **Mute:** post it without its sound.

**Save** makes a new MP4, up to 1080p, on your device. Click **Quick edit** again to change the edit, or **Reset** to go back to the video as it was. For captions, cuts or effects, use **Edit in plan.video** to open it in the [editor](/docs/editor).

## Storage

Your files count toward your plan's storage. You can see how much you use under **Settings** → **Storage**.
