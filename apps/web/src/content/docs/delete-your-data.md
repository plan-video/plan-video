You can remove a single social account, or delete everything.

## Remove a social account

Open **Settings** → **Connections** and remove the account. plan.video stops posting to it and asks the network to end its access.

## Delete your plan.video account

Open **Settings** → **Profile** and choose **Delete account**. This:

- ends plan.video's access to every connected network
- deletes your files in the cloud
- ends your paid plan, if you have one

<Warning>
Deleting your account can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Ask us to delete it

Email [hello@plan.video](mailto:hello@plan.video) from the address you sign in with. We complete requests within 30 days. See the [data deletion page](/data-deletion) for details.
