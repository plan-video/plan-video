Post to your Facebook Pages with text, a link, pictures, a video or a story.

## What you can post

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | 63,206 characters |
| Pictures | Up to 10, 10 MB each |
| Video | One per post |
| Story | One picture or video |

## Good to know

- Posts go to **Pages**, not to a personal profile: Facebook doesn't let apps post to profiles.
- When you connect, Facebook asks which Pages to share with plan.video. Each Page you share becomes its own account, so you can pick which Pages a post goes to.
- You need to be able to **create content** on a Page to post to it.
- A post takes a video or pictures, not both. If it has both, the video goes out.
- A post's link shows as a preview when the post has no pictures or video.
