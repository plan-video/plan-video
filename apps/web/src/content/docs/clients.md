Keep each client in their own [workspace](/docs/workspaces). Two links let them take part without a plan.video account.

## Preview links

Show a client a post before it goes out, and let them comment. Making a preview link is on paid plans. If you move to Free, links you already shared keep working.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the link">
    Click the post on your calendar, then **Share** and **Create link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Anyone with the link can see the post as it will go out, and comment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the comments">
    Open **Share** on the post again. It shows how many comments there are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Stop sharing** ends the link, and removes its comments.

## Client link

Have a client connect their own social accounts to a workspace, without sharing a password with you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the link">
    In **Settings** → **Connections**, find **Client link** and click **Create link**. It's copied for you. You need to be the workspace's owner or an admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Your client opens it, sees your workspace's name, and clicks **Connect** on each network to sign in there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post">
    Their accounts show up in your workspace, ready to post to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A link works for 7 days, and **Client link** shows how many accounts have connected.

- **Turn off** ends the link early, and **New link** replaces it. Accounts already connected stay.
- Each account counts toward your plan's limit, and X is only offered if your plan includes it.
- Pinterest isn't offered, and Bluesky uses **Sign in with Bluesky**, not an app password.
- To remove an account later, click its **×** in **Connections**. Your client can also remove plan.video in the network's own settings.
