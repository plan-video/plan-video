plan.video's video editor runs in your browser, with nothing to install. It needs a computer and a Chromium browser, such as Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc. The rest of plan.video also works on your phone.

## Projects

Your videos live under **Projects** in the dashboard. Click **New project** to start one, and sort them into folders with **New folder**. A project you delete stays in **Trash** for 30 days, and **Restore** brings it back.

## Add to your video

A new project opens on **Drop media here**. Drop a video onto it, click **Import media**, or choose **Start with an empty scene** to begin with text or shapes.

The tabs on the editor's rail hold what you can add:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Assets** | Your project's files. **Add media** brings in more, and **From library** uses your [media library](/docs/media-library). Drag a file onto the timeline or the canvas, or hover it and click **+** to add it at the playhead. |
| **Text** | **Add a text box**, or a ready-made style. Many styles move: they float, pulse, wiggle or flicker for as long as the text is on screen. Hover a style to see it move. |
| **Captions** | Caption styles that play as you look at them. Pick one, then **Generate captions** writes captions from the scene's speech. See [Captions](/docs/captions). |
| **Transitions** | Sixteen, in **Basic**, **Movement**, **Zoom** and **Stylized**: **Dissolve**, **Whip**, **Light leak**, **Glitch** and more, on a clip, between two clips or on every cut. Click one to add it to the selected clip, or to the clip under the playhead. Hover one to see it play. |
| **Effects** | Eighteen, in **Camera**, **Moments** and **Looks**: **Auto zoom**, **Handheld**, **Glitch**, **Glow**, **Vignette** and more, for the selected clip or the one at the playhead. Its settings then show in the inspector. |
| **Layout** | Scene sizes like **Short-form 9:16** and **Square video 1:1** |
| **Extensions** | Add-ons like **Progress bar** and **Killfeed** |

## Change what's selected

Select something, and the inspector shows what it can do:

- **Position, size, speed and volume**, with a keyframe control on the rows that can change over time.
- **Add animation**, **Add effect**, **Add mask** and **Add transition**.
- **Add color grade**, with **Looks**, **Adjust** and **Wheels**, and **Save grade** to use it again.
- **Captions**, with their own tools. See [Captions](/docs/captions) and [Cut the transcript](/docs/cut-the-transcript).

Looking for one setting? Click the search icon at the top of the inspector and type its name, like "opacity" or "volume". Only the matching sections stay.

A bar at the bottom of the canvas changes with your selection too: **Auto-Captions**, **Remove background** and **Fit scene to video**. Undo and redo sit in the timeline's bar, next to the tools.

## See it on each network

The phone button next to the zoom shows your scene the way a network shows it. **Full screen** has **TikTok**, **Instagram Reels**, **Instagram Stories**, **YouTube Shorts** and **Facebook Reels**. **In the feed** has a YouTube player and a post on Facebook, X, Threads, LinkedIn, Bluesky or Pinterest. The app's buttons, name and caption sit over your video, and a dashed line marks the safe zone: keep text and faces inside it so nothing covers them. A video that's the wrong shape for the app shows with the black bars it would get.

You can keep editing while it's on. Choose **Turn off preview** to hide it. Nothing is added to your video.

## Post or save it

- **Schedule post** opens [New post](/docs/create-a-post) with the project attached. The video is made as a Full HD MP4 while you write the post.
- **Export** lets you choose the quality, then **Save to computer** or **Schedule post** with it. See [Export](/docs/export).

## Find your way around

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> to undo, <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> to redo, and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> to split at the playhead. The menu at the top of the rail lists every shortcut under **Keybinds**, and **Help** → **Take the tour** walks you through the editor. To move and resize the editor's sections, see [Editor layout](/docs/preferences#editor-layout).
