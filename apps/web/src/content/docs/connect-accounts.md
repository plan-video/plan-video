plan.video posts to your social accounts for you. To do that, you connect each account once.

## Connect an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    In the dashboard, open **Settings**, then **Connections**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a network">
    Click **Connect** next to the network. If you already have an account connected there, click **Add account** to connect another one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Sign in on the network's page and allow plan.video to post for you. You'll come back to Connections with the account listed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can connect several accounts on the same network, for example two TikTok accounts. In a shared workspace, only its owner and admins can connect or remove accounts. To have a client connect their own, send them a [client link](/docs/clients#client-link).

### Bluesky

Bluesky offers two ways to connect: **Sign in with Bluesky**, or an **app password** made in Bluesky's settings. An app password is used once to sign in and is never stored.

### X

X charges for posting through its API, so X is available on paid plans.

### Instagram, Facebook and Threads

Instagram needs a business or creator account. Facebook asks which of your Pages to share, and each Page becomes its own account. Meta doesn't let apps end their own access, so when you remove one of these accounts, plan.video deletes its login and you remove plan.video in the network's settings yourself if you want to.

## Reconnect an account

Networks sometimes end a login, for example after you change your password. The next post to that account fails and says so. Click **Add account** next to the network and sign in to the same account: the new login replaces the old one, and your scheduled posts stay as they are. If you've used every account on your plan, you'll be asked to free one up first.

**Reconnect** shows in place of **Add account** in two cases: a LinkedIn login that's running out, and an older connection marked **Sign in again to post on schedule**.

<Warning>
LinkedIn logins run out after about 60 days. plan.video warns you a week before, with **Login runs out soon** in Connections, in **Inbox** and by email. Click **Reconnect** before then so scheduled LinkedIn posts still go out.
</Warning>

## Remove an account

Click the **×** on the account in Connections. plan.video also asks the network to end its access. If the network doesn't confirm, you'll be told to remove plan.video from the network's own settings as well.

## How your logins are kept

Your logins are encrypted on plan.video's server, which uses them only to send your posts and refresh the login when it runs out. They're never sent back to your browser.

## Account groups

Group accounts by brand or client, like *Acme* on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, so you can pick them all at once in a new post. Make one with **New group** under **Account groups**, in **Settings** → **Connections**.
