**Bulk tools** turns a batch of files into posts in one go, on paid plans. Find it under **Schedule** in the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop your files">
    Drop up to 50 files anywhere on the page, or click **From library**. Each file becomes its own post or story: an MP4 or MOV of up to 5 minutes, or a JPG or PNG. Your first batch starts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, if you've connected them, and fills your [Auto-schedule](/docs/scheduling#auto-schedule) times if you've set them up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change anything, all at once">
    The panel on the right changes every post as you go, with nothing to confirm. Pick the accounts under **Post to**: each file goes to the ones that take it, so pictures skip YouTube and the videos still go there. Then write one **Caption** for every post that doesn't have its own. A caption can use `{name}`, `{n}` and `{date}`, so each post gets its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when">
    Under **When**, choose how many posts go out a day and at what times, how many days apart, and whether to **Skip weekends**. Or use the next free times in Auto-schedule, or your [best times](/docs/analytics#post-at-your-best-times). The times in the list change straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send them off">
    Click **Schedule # posts**, or press ⌘↵ or Ctrl+Enter. You can also **Save as drafts** or **Post now**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change one post, or a few

- **Type on a row** to write that post's caption, or click its time to change it. What you set on a row stays when you change the panel. Press Enter to move to the next post's caption, and Shift+Enter for a new line.
- **Give a post a title** in the **Title** column, which appears when a post is going to YouTube or Pinterest. Left empty, the post is titled after its file.
- **Paste a column** from a spreadsheet onto a caption or a title to fill it down the rows, starting there. Line breaks inside a caption's cell stay.
- **Drag a post by its handle** to change the order, or choose **Shuffle** from the **⋯** menu. The times follow the rows, apart from any you set by hand. With the keyboard, focus the handle and use the up and down arrows.
- **Select posts** to have the panel change only those, even if you set them by hand. Hold Shift to select a range.
- **Click a post's name** to see its preview and every setting it has.

Your batch stays on this device until you schedule it or choose **Clear all** from the **⋯** menu, so you can refresh or come back later and carry on. Each workspace keeps its own. It doesn't follow you to another device.

Stories go to Instagram and Facebook, with no caption.

## Import captions from a spreadsheet

**Import CSV**, in the **⋯** menu at the top of the page, fills in posts from a file. It matches rows to files you've already added, by file name, so add the files first. A row with no matching file is skipped.

| Column | | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `file` | Required | `launch.mp4` |
| `caption` | | `We're live!` |
| `date` | | `2026-10-01`, `2026/10/01` or `01-10-2026` (day first) |
| `time` | | `9:00`, `09:00` or `9pm` |
| `platforms` | | `tiktok, youtube` |

Columns can be separated by commas, semicolons or tabs.
