A workspace holds one brand's social accounts, account groups, posts, calendar and Vault. Everyone starts with one called *Personal*. Use one per brand or client to keep them apart.

## Switch workspaces

Click the workspace name at the top left of the dashboard and pick another one.

## Make a workspace

Choose **New workspace** in the same menu. A new workspace starts with no accounts, posts or Vault items. Give it a name and a logo on the workspace's **General** page, in **Settings**.

## Invite your team

On Pro and Agency, you can ask people into a workspace, as many as you like. Open the workspace's **General** page, enter their email under **Members**, pick a role and choose **Invite**. Someone without a plan.video account gets an email with a link to join. Someone who has one finds the invite in their workspace menu and Inbox. Each invite's row has its link, to send another way: it only works for the email invited.

People see only the workspaces they're invited to, under **Shared with you** in the workspace menu. Their own workspaces stay theirs.

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, and the only one who renames or deletes the workspace. Its plan applies |
| Admin | Everything in the workspace: posts, connected accounts, settings, and who's in it |
| Member | Write, schedule and post from the workspace's accounts. Can't connect or remove accounts, or invite people |

Change someone's role from the menu beside their name, or take them out with ×. The posts they made stay. To leave a workspace someone shared with you, open its **General** page and choose **Leave**.

If the owner moves to a plan without team members, the workspace pauses for everyone else until they're back on Pro or Agency. Nobody is taken out.

## Delete a workspace

Deleting a workspace removes its accounts, posts and calendar, and everyone you invited to it loses access. Your editor projects aren't affected.
