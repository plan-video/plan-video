When you allow an agent in, it acts as you in plan.video.

## What an agent can do

An agent you allow can:

- see your connected accounts, media library and calendar
- read your Analytics: for each connected account, including YouTube channels, its handle, views, likes and comments, how many posts it made, its best weekdays and hours, and the titles and numbers of up to 8 top posts
- schedule, change and delete posts
- publish posts when you ask

It can't see your network logins, change your plan, or delete your account.

Requests pass through plan.video to your open tab and back; plan.video doesn't log or keep them. What the agent receives is handled by the company that runs it, under its own privacy policy.

## Disconnect an agent

Open **MCP & CLI** in the dashboard. Under **Connected apps**, click **Disconnect** next to the agent. It loses access within about a minute.

You can also remove plan.video from the agent's own settings.

## Good habits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for drafts first">
    Ask your agent to save posts as drafts, check them on your calendar, then schedule them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm before publishing">
    Publishing can't be undone. Most agents ask before they use a tool like `publish_post`; let yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect what you don't use">
    Remove agents you've stopped using from **Connected apps**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
