When you allow an agent in, it acts as you in plan.video.

## What an agent can do

An agent you allow can:

- see your connected accounts, media library and calendar
- read your Analytics: for each connected account, including YouTube channels, its handle, views, likes and comments, how many posts it made, its best weekdays and hours, and the titles and numbers of up to 8 top posts
- schedule, change and delete posts
- publish posts when you ask

plan.video asks you in your open tab before an agent publishes, deletes a post, or schedules one that goes out within 10 minutes or to your TikTok profile. Click **Allow** or **Don't allow**. No answer in time counts as no. Drafts, and posts scheduled further ahead, don't ask: they're on your calendar, where you can change or delete them before they go out.

It can't see your network logins, change your plan, or delete your account.

plan.video handles requests as they arrive, and passes the ones that need you to your open tab; it doesn't log or keep them. What the agent receives is handled by the company that runs it, under its own privacy policy.

## Disconnect an agent

Open the dashboard's [**MCP & CLI** page](https://plan.video/dashboard?dashboard=mcp). Under **Connected apps**, click **Disconnect** next to the agent. It loses access within about a minute.

You can also remove plan.video from the agent's own settings.

## Good habits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for drafts first">
    Ask your agent to save posts as drafts, check them on your calendar, then schedule them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm before publishing">
    Publishing can't be undone, so read what plan.video shows you before you click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect what you don't use">
    Remove agents you've stopped using from **Connected apps**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
