Post to Bluesky with text, pictures or a video.

## What you can post

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Text | 300 characters |
| Pictures | Up to 4. Pictures over 1 MB are made smaller for you. |
| Video | Up to 100 MB and 3 minutes |

## Connect

Use **Sign in with Bluesky**, or an **app password** made in Bluesky's settings under **Privacy and security**. The app password is used once to sign in and isn't stored.

## Plugs

Plugs act on your Bluesky posts once they take off. When a post reaches the number of likes you set, plan.video can **repost** it or **reply** to it, for example with a link. Posts are checked 6, 12 and 18 hours after they go out, and a reply can be 3 to 300 characters. Only plugs turned on before a post went out apply to it, and a post that reaches the number after the last check gets nothing.

Set them up in **Plugs** in the dashboard. Plugs work on Bluesky only.
