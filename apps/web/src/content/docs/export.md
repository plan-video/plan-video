Save your video as a file to keep it or post it somewhere else.

## Formats

| Format | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| MP4 | Posting anywhere |
| MOV | Editing in other apps |
| WebM | The web |
| OGG | Audio only |

Click **Export** to pick a preset, like **Full HD**, **YouTube Shorts** or **TikTok**, or set the resolution, bitrate, codec, frame rate and audio yourself. Then choose:

- **Save to computer** to save the file. When it's done, **Play** watches it, and **Schedule this video** opens [New post](/docs/create-a-post) with it attached.
- **Schedule post** to skip the file and open New post straight away. The video is made with your settings while you write the post.

<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd> exports at the default Full HD. <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd> saves the current frame as a picture.

## Quality

| Plan | Highest quality |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 1080p |
| Paid plans | 4K |

Every plan can export at up to 60 frames per second.

<Tip>
Networks take MP4 or MOV with H.264 video and AAC sound, so presets like **Full HD**, **YouTube Shorts** and **TikTok** can all be scheduled. WebM, OGG and other codecs can only be saved.
</Tip>

The **Schedule post** button beside **Export**, or attaching the project to a new post, makes the video at Full HD.

## Keep editing in another app

To finish an edit in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, click **Export**, then **Open in Premiere Pro or Resolve**, and pick a folder. plan.video saves a new folder there with a timeline file (`.xml`) and a `media` folder holding every clip it uses.

In Premiere Pro, choose **File** → **Import** and pick the `.xml`. In DaVinci Resolve, choose **File** → **Import** → **Timeline**. The sequence opens at your scene's size and frame rate, with each clip cut, placed, sized and cropped as in plan.video, at its speed and volume. If the app asks where a clip is, point it at the `media` folder once and it finds the rest.

Only video, sound and picture clips go over. Text, captions, shapes, effects, colour grades, transitions and keyframes stay in plan.video, and clips are placed where they are at the playhead.
