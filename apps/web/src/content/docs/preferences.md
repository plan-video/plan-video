Make plan.video yours. Language and theme are kept on this device; your time zone follows your account.

## Language

plan.video is in English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Nederlands, Polski, Türkçe, 日本語, 한국어 and 简体中文. It starts in your browser's language.

Change it in the dashboard under **Settings** → **Preferences**, or from the language picker at the bottom of any page on the site.

## Theme

Pick **Dark** or **Light** under **Preferences** → **Appearance**, or with the switch at the bottom of the site. One pick applies everywhere. Until you pick, the site starts light, and the dashboard and editor start dark. The docs follow the same choice; use the switch in the top bar.

## Editor layout

Arrange the video editor however you like:

- **Resize:** drag the line between two sections.
- **Move:** click **Customize layout** on the rail, then drag a section. Drop it on the edge of another section to sit beside it, in the middle to swap the two, or on the edge of the window to run along that whole side.
- **Hide:** while customizing, click the **×** on a section. Its name appears in the bar at the bottom, so one click brings it back. With the inspector hidden, **Export** and **Schedule post** move to the corner of the canvas.
- **Ready-made layouts:** open the menu at the top of the rail, then **View** → **Layout**. **Timeline focus**, **Canvas focus** and **Tall sidebars** are one click away.
- **Your own layouts:** choose **Save current layout…** in the same menu and give it a name. It joins the list, and the **×** beside it removes it. It remembers which sections were hidden too.
- **Start over:** choose **Reset layout** in the same menu.

## Time zone

Your time zone starts as your device's. Change it under **Preferences** → **Time zone**. Scheduling, the calendar and analytics all use it, and posts go out at that time.
