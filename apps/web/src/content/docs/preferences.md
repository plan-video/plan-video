Make plan.video yours. These settings are kept on this device.

## Language

plan.video is in English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Nederlands, Polski, Türkçe, 日本語, 한국어 and 简体中文. It starts in your browser's language.

Change it in the dashboard under **Settings** → **Preferences**, or from the language picker at the bottom of any page on the site.

## Theme

Pick **Dark** or **Light** under **Preferences**, or with the switch at the bottom of the site. The docs follow the same choice; use the switch in the top bar.

## Time zone

Your time zone starts as your device's. Change it under **Preferences** → **Time zone**. Scheduling, the calendar and analytics all use it, and posts go out at that time.
