Edit your video by editing its words. Open the **Transcript** panel in the editor once your video has captions.

## Remove pauses

Click **Remove pauses** to cut the silent gaps between words.

## Remove filler words

Click **Remove filler words** to cut words like *um* and *uh*.

## Cut words you pick

Select words in the transcript and cut them. The video, audio and captions are all cut together.

<Tip>
Every cut can be undone, so try the automatic cuts first and fix anything you'd rather keep.
</Tip>
