Edit your video by editing its words. Once your video has captions, select them and find **Transcript** in the inspector.

## Remove pauses

Click **Remove pauses** to cut the silent gaps between words.

## Remove filler words

Click **Remove filler words** to cut words like *um* and *uh*.

## Cut words you pick

Click a word to pick it, drag or shift-click for a run of words, and <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click to add more. Then click **Cut # words from the video**, or press <kbd>Delete</kbd>. The video, audio and captions are all cut together.

<Tip>
Every cut can be undone, so try the automatic cuts first and fix anything you'd rather keep.
</Tip>
