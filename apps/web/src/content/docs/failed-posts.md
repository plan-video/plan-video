Sometimes a network turns a post down, or can't be reached. Here's what plan.video does, and what you can do.

## What plan.video does

- **It tries again.** If a send might work later, like when a network is busy, plan.video tries again after 2, 10 and 30 minutes.
- **It doesn't post twice.** If plan.video can't tell whether a post went out, it marks it failed instead of risking a second copy, and asks you to check the network.
- **It skips posts that are too late.** A post more than 24 hours past its time isn't sent.

## Find failed posts

Open the **Calendar**, switch to **List** and choose **Failed**, which shows while any post has failed. Each post says why it failed and what to do, like *Connect X in Connections, then post it.*

Failed posts also show in **Inbox**, and you get an email, unless it was a **Post now** that failed in front of you on its first try.

## Send it again

Fix what the message says, then click **Retry**. When the network might already have the upload, the button says **Check again** instead, so the post isn't sent twice.

## Common reasons

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The account needs to be reconnected">
    The network ended plan.video's login. Sign in to the account again in **Settings** → **Connections**, then retry. See [Connect accounts](/docs/connect-accounts#reconnect-an-account).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The video doesn't fit the network">
    Check its length, size and shape against [the network's limits](/docs/networks).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The caption is too long">
    Shorten the caption for that network, then retry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="X's limit was reached">
    Each plan posts to X up to a set number of times in any 30 days, and fewer of those with a link. A post past that isn't sent, and its error says when there's room again. See [X](/docs/x). Past 50 posts in a day, a post isn't a failure: it waits and goes out on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Free plan's 10 waiting posts were used">
    On the Free plan, each account can have 10 scheduled posts waiting at a time, and **New post** stops you there. A post that gets past that anyway isn't sent at its time. Click **Upgrade** for unlimited posts, and it goes out within a day of its time on its own. Or click **Post now** to send it straight away.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
