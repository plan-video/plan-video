Sometimes a network turns a post down, or can't be reached. Here's what plan.video does, and what you can do.

## What plan.video does

- **It tries again.** If a send might work later, like when a network is busy, plan.video retries up to 4 times over about 40 minutes.
- **It doesn't post twice.** If plan.video can't tell whether a post went out, it marks it failed instead of risking a second copy, and asks you to check the network.
- **It skips posts that are too late.** A post more than 24 hours past its time isn't sent.

## Find failed posts

Open **Posts** and choose **Failed**. Each post says why it failed and what to do, like *Connect X in Connections, then post it.*

## Send it again

Fix what the message says, then click **Retry**. When the network might already have the upload, the button says **Check again** instead, so the post isn't sent twice.

## Common reasons

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The account needs to be reconnected">
    The network ended plan.video's login. Click **Reconnect** in **Settings** → **Connections**, then retry. See [Connect accounts](/docs/connect-accounts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The video doesn't fit the network">
    Check its length, size and shape against [the network's limits](/docs/networks).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The caption is too long">
    Shorten the caption for that network, then retry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="X's daily limit was reached">
    You can post up to 50 times a day to X. Posts over that fail until the next day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Free plan's 10 waiting posts were used">
    On the Free plan, each account can have 10 scheduled posts waiting at a time. A post scheduled past that isn't sent at its time. Click **Upgrade** for unlimited posts, and it goes out within a day of its time on its own. Or click **Post now** to send it straight away.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
