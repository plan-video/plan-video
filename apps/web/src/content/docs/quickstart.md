Go from a new account to a scheduled post.

<Tip>
Want a look first? [The demo](/try) opens the dashboard and editor with sample posts. Nothing in it is saved or sent.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [plan.video](https://plan.video) and sign in with your email. You'll land on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a network">
    Open **Settings**, then **Connections**, and click **Connect** next to the network you want to post to. You'll sign in on the network's own page and come back to plan.video. See [Connect accounts](/docs/connect-accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your post">
    Click **New post**, pick the accounts to post to, add a video or pictures, and write a caption. Each network gets its own preview, and you can change the caption for one network without touching the others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a time">
    Under **When**, pick **Next free time**, a quick time like **Tomorrow**, or **Date and time**. Then click **Schedule**, or **Auto-schedule** for a free time. To send it straight away, click **Post now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your calendar">
    Your post shows up on the **Calendar**. Click it to edit or delete it. In week view, you can also drag it to a new time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Times follow your time zone, which you can change under **Preferences**. See [Language and theme](/docs/preferences).
</Note>
