Once your agent is connected, ask it in your own words. Here are some ideas.

## Plan

```text
What's on my calendar next week?
```

```text
When do my TikTok posts do best? Schedule my next three videos at those times.
```

## Schedule

```text
Schedule launch.mp4 from my media library to TikTok and YouTube for Friday at 6pm. Title it "We're live" on YouTube.
```

```text
Write five posts about our summer sale for X and Bluesky, keep each under 280 characters, and spread them over next week at 9am. Save them as drafts so I can check them.
```

```text
Post a reminder to LinkedIn every Monday at 8am for the next four weeks.
```

## Change

```text
Move all of Thursday's posts to Friday, same times.
```

```text
Copy last week's best TikTok post and schedule it again for Sunday evening.
```

## Publish

```text
Publish the draft about the new feature now.
```

<Tip>
Your agent can only attach files that are in your [media library](/docs/media-library). Upload them there first.
</Tip>
