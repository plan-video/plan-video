The **Calendar** shows every post: drafts, scheduled posts and posts that have gone out.

## Views

| View | Key | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week | <kbd>W</kbd> | A week by the hour |
| Month | <kbd>M</kbd> | A whole month |
| List | <kbd>L</kbd> | Upcoming posts in order |

## Move a post

In week view, drag a post to another day or time. The post goes out at its new time.

## Edit, copy or delete

Click a post to open it. From there you can edit it, copy it, move it to drafts or delete it. Repeating posts also show controls to pause, skip or end the series.

## Failed posts

If a post didn't go out, it shows as failed. See [Failed posts](/docs/failed-posts).
