The **Calendar** shows every post: drafts, scheduled posts and posts that have gone out.

## Views

| View | Key | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week | <kbd>W</kbd> | A week by the hour |
| Month | <kbd>M</kbd> | A whole month |
| List | <kbd>L</kbd> | Every post, with what's coming first and earlier posts below |

The calendar opens in month view; change that under **Preferences** → **Open the calendar in**. Press <kbd>T</kbd> for today, and the arrow keys to go back and forward. In month view, click a day to open its week.

The sidebar lists your posts by status: **Scheduled**, **Drafts** and **Posted**, with **Failed** appearing when a post didn't go out. Each opens the list showing only those posts.

## Move a post

In week view, drag a post to another day or time. The post goes out at its new time. Posts that have gone out stay where they are.

## Edit, share or delete

Click a post to open it. From there you can **Edit**, **Share** a [preview link](/docs/clients#preview-links) or **Delete** it, and the **…** menu has **Quick edit**, **Copy caption** and **Move to drafts**. A post that has gone out shows **Post again**. Repeating posts also show controls to pause or end the series, and **Delete** offers **Skip this one**.

## Failed posts

If a post didn't go out, it shows as failed. See [Failed posts](/docs/failed-posts).
