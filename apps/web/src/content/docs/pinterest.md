Post Pins with pictures or a video to the boards on your Pinterest account.

## What you can post

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Description | 500 characters |
| Title | 100 characters |
| Pictures | Up to 5 in one Pin |
| Video | One per Pin, up to 5 minutes and 500 MB |

Pinterest needs a picture or a video, so words alone don't go out there.

## Good to know

- Each post needs a **board**. Pick one of your boards under the post's Pinterest settings, or type a new name and plan.video makes the board when the post goes out. Secret boards can't be posted to.
- A Pin's **link** is where it opens when someone clicks it.
- To choose a video's cover, pick a frame in the post.
- Your Pinterest login renews by itself while plan.video posts for you. After two months without a post, connect the account again in **Settings** → **Connections**.
- Pinterest has no way for an app to end its own access, so when you remove the account, plan.video deletes its login and you remove plan.video in Pinterest's settings yourself if you want to.
