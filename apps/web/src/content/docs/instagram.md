Post to an Instagram professional account with pictures, a video, a carousel or a story.

## What you can post

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Caption | 2,200 characters, up to 30 hashtags |
| Pictures | Up to 10 in a carousel, shaped between 4:5 and 1.91:1 |
| Video | Goes out as a Reel, up to 3 minutes and 300 MB |
| Story | One picture or video, up to 60 seconds and 100 MB, with no caption |

## Good to know

- Instagram needs a **business or creator account**. A personal account can switch for free in Instagram's settings, under **Account type and tools**.
- Every Instagram post needs a picture or a video: there are no text-only posts.
- Several pictures go out as one **carousel**. Instagram crops every picture to the shape of the first.
- A picture outside 4:5 to 1.91:1 is cropped from the middle when it goes out. The post says so under the pictures. To choose what stays, click **Crop it**: the editor opens on the nearest shape Instagram takes.
- Choose whether a Reel also shows in your **feed**, and add up to three **collaborators**, in the post's Instagram settings.
- Instagram limits how many posts an app can make for one account in a day. Past that, posts wait and go out on their own once they can. A Reel or carousel still waiting after 2 hours fails, and you can send it again.
- Your Instagram login lasts **60 days** and renews by itself while plan.video posts or reads your numbers. After two months without either, connect the account again in **Settings** → **Connections**.
